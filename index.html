Night Shift Studio

Night Shift Studio produces long, calm "study with me / work with me" videos for the YouTube channel Night Shift — Work With Me: Pomodoro timers, rain, fireplace and lofi or jazz music for focused late-night work.

The Night Shift Studio app is an internal publishing tool. It signs in only with the channel owner's own Google account to upload, schedule and manage videos on that one channel.
